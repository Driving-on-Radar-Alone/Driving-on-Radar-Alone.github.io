What this page holds

This page accompanies the submission. It carries the onboard footage from the real-vehicle campaign: every run that was driven, in full, with nothing cut for presentation.

Each clip below shows all four camera angles at once — forward (F), rearward (R) and the two cabin views (SL, SR) — assembled from the synchronised originals without reframing or cropping.
